Three lines of integration

app.post("/v1/chat/completions",
  requirePayment({ amount: parseUsdc("0.005"), escrow, service, chain: ARC_TESTNET }),
  async (req, res) => res.json(await yourHandler(req)),
);

The middleware verifies an EIP-712 signed claim in <50ms locally, sets req.arc402Claim for your handler, and queues the claim for batched on-chain settlement. No payment-channel setup, no API keys, no per-call tx.
